# Title screen pictures

The rooms behind the title screen's menu, in the order they cycle (five seconds each, with a slow pan).

The first four are the original workshops (build 230). The owner generated them. Before they went in:

- **Logos pixelated.** Every maker's and sponsor's logo was pixelated out with a 26 px mosaic: RAM, Ferrari (signs, shields, the windscreen banner, the "Ferrari Club Italia" boards), Pirelli, Shell, Agip, Tesla and Powerpack.
- **Garbled text pixelated.** The few bits of garbled lettering the generator left were pixelated the same way.
- **Resized and converted.** The originals are about 2800×1540 JPEGs. These are 1920×1072 WebP at quality 82.

Some tiny marks are left, too small to read: a car's bonnet badge, wheel centre caps.
