# Title screen pictures

The four rooms behind the title screen's menu, in the order they cycle (five seconds each, with a slow pan):

| File | Room | Source file on `assets/car-profiles-and-landing` |
|---|---|---|
| `workshop-pickup.webp` | a pickup on the hoist in a back-street workshop | `assets/landing/dodge-ram.jpg` |
| `garage-f355.webp` | an F355 on stands with its wheels off | `assets/landing/ferrari-f355-alt.jpg` |
| `service-bay-ev.webp` | an electric car's service bay, its drive unit on a dolly | `assets/landing/tesla-model-y.jpg` |
| `pit-f355.webp` | the F355 in a race pit | `assets/landing/ferrari-f355.jpg` |

The owner generated them in a pixel-art style. Before they went in (build 229):
- **Logos pixelated.** Every maker's and sponsor's logo was pixelated out with a 26 px mosaic: RAM, Ferrari (signs,
  shields, the windscreen banner, the "Ferrari Club Italia" boards), Pirelli, Shell, Agip, Tesla and Powerpack.
- **Garbled text pixelated.** The few bits of garbled lettering the generator left were pixelated the same way.
- **Resized and converted.** The originals are about 2800x1540 JPEGs, 11 MB in all. These are 1920 px wide WebP at
  quality 82, 0.8 MB in all.

Some tiny marks are left, too small to read: a car's bonnet badge, wheel centre caps.
