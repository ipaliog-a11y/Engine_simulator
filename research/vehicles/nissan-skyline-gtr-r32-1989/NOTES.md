# Nissan Skyline GT-R (R32, 1989)

Queued in batch-2 **wave 1**. Research filled in `vehicle.json`.

## Sources

- **Engine / body / dims:** Nissan Heritage Collection BNR32 sheet — 1430 kg curb, 280 PS @ 6800, 353 Nm @ 4400, 4545×1755×1340 mm, wb 2615, tyres 225/50R16.
- **Cd / gearing:** Secondary GT-R chassis tables and FS5R30A ratio sheets (3.214…0.752, fd 4.111). Cd 0.40 and ratios are well-repeated; Cd stays in `unsure`.
- **Performance:** Period 0–100 ~5.0–5.3 s; ~250 km/h limited — both unsure.

## Chassis

`coupe` with `drive: awd`, same pattern as the R34 already in the game.

## Engine

New preset `Nissan RB26DETT (Skyline GT-R R32)` — same bore/stroke/compression as the R34 RB26 entry, but that preset’s torque figure is not the R32 sheet’s 353 Nm, so they stay separate.

## Confidence

**high** for mass, power, geometry, gears. Do not raise the gentlemen’s-agreement 280 PS to “make” a road-test time.
