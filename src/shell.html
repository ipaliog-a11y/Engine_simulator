ENGINE SOUND
Synthesised from the firing pulses (firing order, one exhaust per bank; lumpy idle from wild cams). Plays on the DYNO tab, and on the TRACK tab with the lap replay.
ENGINE SOUND MODEL
SYNTH: firing pulses through modelled pipes (light). GAS: the cylinders, runners and exhaust simulated as gas, after engine-sim by Ange Yaghi, with a recorded exhaust (heavier; a V12 uses about half a CPU core). Four-strokes, two-strokes and rotaries all have their own gas model.
